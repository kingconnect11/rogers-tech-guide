How the panel works

Four panelists (Roger, Philip, Michael, George). The founder pitches for ten minutes, then twenty minutes of questions, asked the way a screening committee asks them. Each panelist fills in one sheet during the pitch and finishes it in the five minutes after. The founder receives all four within 48 hours.

Sold alone: $750 a founder. Included at the end of the Full Program. Offered to programs as a demo day rehearsal.
