Pitch an Angel, for programs

Get your whole cohort ready to pitch, in one afternoon.

A two-hour pitch workshop with Roger King, founder of Bay Angels, for accelerators, incubators, university programs and small business centers.

What happens

  • Roger's five rules for an angel pitch, with real examples of what lands and what loses the room.
  • Every founder rewrites their first 30 seconds, live.
  • Three founders pitch for five minutes and get Roger's feedback in front of the cohort.
  • The ten questions angels always ask, and how to answer them short.

What every founder leaves with

  • A personal pitch workbook, built from their own company before the day.
  • A rewritten opening.
  • A timing plan for their demo day slot.
  • The option of a one-on-one session with Roger at a program rate.

Format

Two hours, live on Zoom or in person in the Bay Area. Up to 20 founders. Pre-seed to Series A. Founders fill in a short form a week before, so their workbooks are ready on the day.

About Roger

Roger King founded Bay Angels in 1997, one of Silicon Valley's longest-running angel groups, and has heard hundreds of pitches since. He has hosted and judged pitch competitions in Havana, Beijing, Prague, Seoul and Tokyo, and his views have appeared in Forbes, Fortune and on CNN. Pitch an Angel is his independent coaching practice and is separate from Bay Angels.

Prep, two weeks out

  1. Agree the date, the price and who pays. Send an invoice for half (Philip sets up the payment link).
  2. Send the program a short form for founders with these questions: name, company, what they do, stage, next pitch (event and date), slot length in minutes, how much they are raising, their strongest proof, and their current opening line. The answers paste straight into the workbook maker.
  3. One week out: paste the founders' form answers into the workbook maker and print or save every workbook in one go. For a deeper one, use the ChatGPT workbook prompt.
  4. Pick the three founders who pitch live, with the program director.
  5. Day before: ask ChatGPT to prep you on the three, with the "Prep me for a call" prompt.

After

  1. Same day: thank the program director, send the second invoice, and ask for one sentence we can quote.
  2. Within two days: email every founder their workbook (if not sent before), and the program rate for a session.
  3. Ask the director: "Who else runs a cohort like yours?" That is the next workshop.
