Pitch an Angel, live on Zoom

Pitch Clinic with Roger King

Once a month, six founders pitch for five minutes and get Roger's honest feedback, live. Everyone else watches and learns.

[Thursday, date] · 4:00 to 5:30 pm Pacific · Zoom

Pitch seat

$149

Six seats. Pitch for five minutes, get seven minutes of feedback from Roger and a guest, and take home your notes. Credited toward a Single Session if you book within 30 days.

Watch

$49

Up to fifteen people. Hear six pitches and the feedback on each. Most founders learn as much from watching as from pitching.

Who it is for

Pre-seed and seed founders with a pitch event or investor meetings in the next three months, and anyone who wants to hear what an investor hears.

What to expect

  • A short opening from Roger on what makes the first 90 seconds work.
  • Six pitches, five minutes each, timed.
  • Candid, specific feedback on each: what landed, what lost the room, what to fix first.
  • The questions an angel would ask next.

Good to know

  • Pitches are heard by up to 20 people. Do not share anything confidential.
  • With your written permission, the feedback segments may be recorded for Pitch an Angel's course. You can say no and still pitch.
  • Feedback only. No introductions to investors, and no effect on any Bay Angels application.

What Philip sets up (about an hour, once)

  1. Two Stripe payment links: "Pitch Clinic seat, $149" (limit 6) and "Pitch Clinic, watch, $49". Stripe charges a card fee per sale and no monthly fee.
  2. A Zoom meeting with registration, so only people who paid get the link.
  3. A short form for pitch seats: name, company, one line on what they do, deck link, and recording consent (yes or no).
  4. This sign-up page on pitchanangel.com.

Filling it

  • One LinkedIn post from Roger ten days before, and one the day before.
  • A personal note to everyone on Roger's "To ask" list who is not ready for a $495 session.
  • Founders who filled in the intake but did not book.
